## Featured Project

### [Taipei YouBike Network and Road Risk Analysis](https://github.com/kuku0508/YouBike-Road-Risk-Analysis)

A reproducible analysis combining large-scale YouBike trip data, reconstructed road-level cycling exposure, and traffic crash records.

- Built a data-processing pipeline beginning with **109,713,899 parsed trips**
- Constructed a directed OD network with **2,782 stations** and **1,125,519 edges**
- Completed **1,100,919 BRouter routes** to estimate cycling exposure on road microsegments
- Reconstructed **46,586 crash entities** and formally matched **39,711 crashes** to road microsegments
- Applied network analysis, clustering, geospatial matching, data-quality checks, and automated tests
- Documented analytical assumptions, exclusion states, research limitations, and reproducibility decisions

The project uses data to narrow down road environments worth further investigation. It is not intended to provide a definitive ranking of Taipei’s most dangerous roads.

## How I Approach Analysis

I care about more than producing a final chart or model. I try to make each analysis:

- **Traceable:** Important assumptions, transformations, and decisions are documented
- **Reproducible:** Code, configurations, validation records, and necessary data fingerprints are preserved
- **Interpretable:** Results are explained together with their practical meaning and limitations
- **Careful with uncertainty:** Confirmed facts, reasonable inferences, and unresolved questions are kept separate
